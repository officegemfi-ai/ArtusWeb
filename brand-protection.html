Brand protection

Your excess never appears where it should not.

You decide where a lot may be sold. TREKSTONE buys it, takes title, and moves it only through the regions and channels you have approved, under contract and on the record.

The controls

Four controls on every lot.

Geo-restricted channels

Each lot carries the list of regions where it may be sold. Buyers accept those limits in writing before any product moves.

Vetted buyers under contract

Every buyer is reviewed before the first offer and signs resale restrictions: where they may sell, and through which channels.

NFR marking and de-branding

Chain of custody for every pair
