How it works

A controlled path for every lot.

From intake to verified destination, each step is contracted, brand-safe and documented.

Discuss a lot

Intake and grade

We receive the lot and grade every pair by condition and brand sensitivity, A to D.

  • ConditionFrom retail-ready to samples and specials.
  • Brand sensitivityYour brand rules decide what each grade may do next.
  • Lot recordUnits received are the baseline every outcome reconciles to.

Route

Each grade is assigned to a vetted, geo-controlled channel under contract.

  • Approved regionsOnly the markets you have cleared for the lot.
  • Approved channelsResale, donation or recycling, decided by grade.
  • Contracts firstResale restrictions are signed before product moves.

Protect

NFR marking, de-branding and resale restrictions are enforced at every hand-off.

  • NFR markingApplied when the deal terms require it.
  • De-brandingApplied when the deal terms require it.
  • Resale restrictionsRegion and channel limits travel with the goods.

Document

Chain of custody, destinations and outcomes are captured for your records.

  • Chain of custodyPartner, carrier, date and destination for every pair.
  • Outcome ledgerThe resale, donation and recycling split.
  • CertificatesDestination and recycling certificates, filed with the lot.

Grading

Every grade has a controlled market.

Every pair is graded on arrival, and every grade has its own approved route to market.

Start with one lot

One lot, 90 days.

A single lot is enough to prove brand control, value recovery and audit-ready reporting, with no disruption to your primary channels.

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Scope and controls

    Confirm the lot, grading logic, geo-restrictions and brand rules.

  2. Weeks 3–4

    Channels and contracts

    Approve vetted destinations and resale-restriction agreements.

  3. Weeks 5–8

    Controlled movement

    Process the lot with proof of destination at every step.

  4. Weeks 9–12

    Results and scale

    Recovery value, reporting and a recommendation for scale.

  • Success criteria
  • Brand control held
  • Value recovered
