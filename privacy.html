Privacy Policy

How we handle personal information on this website. In short: no cookies, no tracking, and we only receive what you choose to send us.

Last updated: November 2026

In short

  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Our host logs technical details, such as your IP address, to deliver the site securely.
  • Photos and video come from our own server.
  • We only receive your details when you write to us.

When you visit this website

Like every website, ours is delivered by a hosting provider. To send you the pages and keep the site secure, the provider processes technical details in its server logs: your IP address, the date and time, the page or file requested, and basic information about your browser and device.

We do not use these details to identify you and we do not combine them with other information.

No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or other tracking tools. Nothing is stored on your device.

The photos and video on the site come from our own server, and the text uses fonts already on your device, so loading a page sends nothing to third parties.

When you contact us

You can email us, call us or use the “Discuss a lot” form. The form sends nothing through this website: it opens your own email app with your request filled in, and you decide whether to send it.

When you write to us we receive what you choose to tell us: your name, company, email address, phone number if you give one, and what you tell us about your inventory. We use it to answer you, to prepare a grading and routing plan, and to stay in touch about your request.

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them for that purpose and for our business records.

The client portal

Clients reach their reporting through a separate portal with its own login. The portal is not part of this website. How information is handled there is set out in our agreements with each client.

Who we share information with

We use service providers to host this website and to run our email. They process information for us and only for that purpose.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for advertising. We may disclose information where the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it. Write to soureno@trekstone.net and we will reply.

Depending on where you live, for example in California, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the law may give you further rights, including the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Visitors from outside the United States

TREKSTONE is based in the United States. If you contact us from another country, your information is processed in the United States.

Children

This website is written for businesses. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
