How it works

A controlled path for every lot.

From intake to verified destination, each step is contracted, brand-safe and documented.

See how it works

  1. Intake and grade

    We receive the lot and grade every pair by condition and brand sensitivity, A to D.

  2. Route

    Each grade is assigned to a vetted, geo-controlled channel under contract.

  3. Protect

    NFR marking, de-branding and resale restrictions are enforced at every hand-off.

  4. Document

    Chain of custody, destinations and outcomes are captured for your records.

Why now

Destroying excess is ending. Proving where it goes is the new standard.

Three forces now apply to unsold footwear and apparel at the same time. Each asks the same question: where did the product go, and can you prove it?

What it means for brands

EU

From 19 July 2026

A ban on destroying unsold goods

Large companies may no longer destroy unsold apparel, clothing accessories or footwear placed on the EU market.

California

Phasing in from 2026

Producer responsibility for textiles

SB 707 makes producers of apparel and footwear responsible for collection, repair, reuse and recycling.

Reporting

Every year

Disclosures that hold up

ESRS E5 and GRI 306 ask what happens to products and materials, with figures that trace to source documents.
