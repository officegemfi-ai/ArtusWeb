What we ask of every buyer

Questions

Before you apply.

Who can apply?

Companies that buy footwear, apparel or accessories for resale: wholesalers, distributors, retailers, online resellers and exporters. We also work with donation and recycling partners.

What happens after I apply?

We review your details and may contact you with questions. Approved buyers receive a buyer agreement to sign, then start receiving offers that match their profile.

What does an offer include?

The product category, grade, quantity and location, together with any resale conditions, such as the regions and channels where the goods may be sold.

How is my information used?

To review your application and to send you offers, and for nothing else. The details are in our Privacy Policy.
