When you visit this website

Like every website, ours is delivered by a hosting provider. To send you the pages and keep the site secure, the provider processes technical details in its server logs: your IP address, the date and time, the page or file requested, and basic information about your browser and device.

We do not use these details to identify you and we do not combine them with other information.

No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or other tracking tools. Nothing is stored on your device.

The photos and fonts on the site come from our own site, so loading a page sends nothing to third parties.

When you send an enquiry

The “Discuss a lot” form sends what you enter to TREKSTONE: your name, company and email address, your phone number if you give one, and what you tell us about your inventory. You can also email or call us.

We use this to answer you, to prepare a grading and routing plan, and to stay in touch about your request. We keep enquiries for as long as we need them for that purpose and for our business records.

When you apply as a buyer

The buyer sign-up form collects what we need to review a company before sending it offers:

  • your company: name, country, city, website, type of business, years trading and team size;
  • your contact details: name, job title, email address, phone number and how you prefer to be reached;
  • what you buy: categories, segments, grades, order size and how often you buy;
  • where and how you sell: regions, countries, sales channels, platforms and where goods should ship to;
  • the confirmations you give when you send the form, and anything you add in the notes.

We use this to review your application, to decide whether to approve you as a buyer, to send you offers that match your profile, and to manage our dealings with you. We do not ask for licence or tax numbers in the form.

We keep buyer details while your application is open and for as long as you remain a registered buyer. You can ask us at any time to stop sending offers or to delete your details; we will keep only what we need for our business records or are required by law to keep.

When we report to a brand on where its goods went, buyers are identified by a masked reference, not by name.

How form submissions are stored

Enquiries and buyer applications are stored in a database run for us by a service provider, Supabase, in the United States. To protect the forms from misuse, we also record when a form was sent and a scrambled version of the sender’s IP address that cannot be turned back into the address.

The client portal

Clients reach their reporting through a separate portal with its own login. The portal is not part of this website. How information is handled there is set out in our agreements with each client.

Who we share information with

We use service providers to host this website, to store form submissions and to run our email. They process information for us and only for that purpose.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for advertising. We may disclose information where the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it. Write to soureno@trekstone.net and we will reply.

Depending on where you live, for example in California, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the law may give you further rights, including the right to complain to a data protection authority.

TREKSTONE is based in the United States. If you contact us or apply from another country, your information is processed in the United States.
